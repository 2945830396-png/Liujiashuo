## Activities Completed

During this week's practical session, I began developing my startup website using Joomla CMS. I selected an environmental sustainability startup called **EcoGo**, which focuses on eco-friendly delivery solutions and promoting sustainable business practices.

First, I installed Joomla and explored the administrator dashboard to understand the basic structure of the content management system. I learned how to navigate through articles, menus, media, and template settings.

I customised the default Joomla website to better represent my startup idea. The website name was changed to **EcoGo**, and the site description was updated to reflect the company's mission. I also modified the template settings by applying a green colour scheme to match the environmental theme of the business.

Next, I created several articles to form the foundation of the website. These included the following pages:

* Home
* About Us
* Services
* Contact

After creating the articles, I linked them to the **Main Menu** so that visitors could easily navigate between different sections of the website. I also added sustainability-related images to make the website more visually appealing and relevant to the startup concept.

## Challenges Encountered

One challenge I faced was understanding the relationship between articles and menu items in Joomla. Initially, I was unsure why newly created pages did not automatically appear on the website. After further exploration, I learned that menu items must be created separately and linked to the corresponding articles.

Another challenge involved customising the template without affecting the overall functionality of the website. Through experimentation and online resources, I gained a better understanding of Joomla's template system.
